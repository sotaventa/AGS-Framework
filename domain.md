    3 ## Core Philosophy: The Post-Marketing Era
    4 Growth has shifted from **Attention Capture** (persuasion-led) to **Decision Qualification** (logic-led). The primary customer is no longer a human browsing a screen, but an autonomous agent
      performing high-speed evaluations.
    5
    6 ## The Three Laws of Autonomous Growth
    7 1. **Visibility is determined by machine readability**: If machines cannot interpret your data, you do not exist.
    8 2. **Preference is determined by verifiable performance**: Measurable reliability replaces perceived value.
    9 3. **Growth emerges from total system integration**: Silos are the enemy; growth is an architectural capability.
   10
   11 ## The Four Pillars of AGS
   12 1. **Agentic Commerce (The Transaction Engine)**: Focuses on **Agentic Commerce Optimisation (ACO)**. Unlike AEO (Agentic Engine Optimisation), which is purely content-focused, ACO is the
      structural engineering of digital infrastructure (WebMCP/UCP, 1PD liquidity, schema design) to ensure autonomous discovery and execution.
   13 2. **Operational Alpha (The Source of Advantage)**: Real-time, verifiable performance signals (pricing, fulfilment) that drive machine selection.
   14 3. **Personalised AI Agents (The Distribution Layer)**: Internal and external agents managing intent and operational response.
   15 4. **Intelligent Governance (The Trust Layer)**: Programmable trust, data integrity, and identity verification.
   16
   17 ## Key Metrics & Success Indicators
   18 - **MPI (Machine Preference Index)**: Objective measure of selection probability within a model.
   19 - **SoM (Share of Model)**: Percentage of recommendations within an AI model's decision weights.
   20 - **CPAA (Cost Per Agent Acquisition)**: Total investment in data infrastructure and signal integrity required to become "selectable."
   21 - **ARS (Agentic Readiness Score)**: Benchmark of an organisation's maturity across discovery, decisioning, and execution.
